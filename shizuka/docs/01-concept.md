# 01. Concept

**Shizuka (静か)**: aged paper and sumi ink. Wabi-sabi as an interface.

- **Peace:** emptiness, off-white, water-and-smoke forms (ink bloom), paper tones, something that feels like it has a history.
- **Discomfort:** numbers that fade away, ink that escapes control, time passing.
- **One primary:** the kanji, plain and faded. Default shu `#A8473A`.
- **Hero object:** one ink bloom per screen.
- **Basics stay basic:** straight lines, simple buttons, plain text. Decoration is reserved for showcase pieces (`Decor` in the Remotion parts, `.decor` in the CSS).
- Full reference: `../SHIZUKA.md`.

## Myth
An old journal that became an interface. It does not just serve: it makes you feel the weight of time and accept the unfinished.

## It is not
Not tech, not sterile "clean", not chrome, not lime. That was REV.02; it was replaced.
