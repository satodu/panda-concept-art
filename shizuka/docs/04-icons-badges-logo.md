# 04. Icons, badges and logo

- **App icon:** super-rounded square (26%) of paper, a small ink bloom and the kanji 静 in the primary.
- **Badge:** paper card, ink in the center, kanji 間, title in Inter Tight 200 with a full stop.
- **Logo:** `SHIZUKA.` Inter Tight 200, wide tracking + ink bloom. Minimum kanji size 16px.
- **UI icons:** 1.25px stroke, round cap, 24px. Symbols: `○ ∿ ⌂ ◐ |`.

```bash
node tools/make-asset.mjs --concept shizuka --type icon --png
node tools/make-asset.mjs --concept shizuka --type badge --title "quiet" --png
```
Remotion: `Shizuka-Logo` (the ink spreads, the word settles, the kanji appears).
