# 07. AI prompts

Full library: `../PROMPTS.md` (144 prompts). For UI in code use the **master prompt** (section 19 of `SHIZUKA.md`).

**Image base**
`wabi-sabi interface, aged off-white washi paper with visible fibre and soft stains, a real black sumi ink bloom with wisps and tendrils spreading on wet paper, organic, not concentric rings, numbers partly rubbed away, one plain faded red kanji, huge empty space, peaceful and quietly uncomfortable --ar 16:9`

**Rules**
1. Generate without text; kanji and words are added later in HTML/SVG.
2. Ask for "partly rubbed away numerals" to get the fading-numbers effect.
3. Negatives: concentric rings, target-like circles, hanko seals, stamp boxes, chrome, glossy 3D, neon, big shadow, flat white paper, fake kanji.
4. People: small, from the side or behind, in an empty paper-screen tatami room.
