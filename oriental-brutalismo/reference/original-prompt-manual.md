**01.** **EDITORIAL AND VISUAL ART DIRECTION.**

Contemporary editorial graphics fusing a minimalist UI Design System, architectural Brutalism and avant-garde Japanese editorial design.

**Structure \& Layout:** A modular composition inspired by Bento Grid with generous spacing (breathing room / the 'Ma' concept). Floating geometric panels with ultrathin 1px translucent borders (1px hairline rules) and solid blocks of deconstructed exposed concrete.

**Token \& Color Palette:**

1. **Canvas Background (surface-0):** Deep matte charcoal (#090a0f / #0b0c10).
2. **Card Surfaces (surface-1):** Matte dark graphite (#14171f).
3. **Typography and Lines:** Crisp off-white (#FFFFFF / #EEEEEE) and industrial grays.
4. **Signature Accent Color:** Intense vibrant red (#E63946 / vermilion) applied sparingly in deconstructions, highlights and emphasis.

**Typography \& Oriental Graphics:**

1. **Titles:** Heavy geometric sans-serif (Archivo Black / Cabinet Grotesk style) ending with a dry full stop (e.g. "NAME.", "SYSTEM.").
2. **Oriental Layer:** Narrow vertical columns of Japanese characters (Kanji/Katakana in Gothic/Mincho style) acting as structural rulers.
3. **Micro-texts:** Compact monospace technical typography for tags and coordinates.

**UI Micro-details \& Decoration:**

1. Rhythmic patterns of technical crosses (+ + + +) and micro-dot meshes (dot matrix).
2. Minimalist red oriental seal stamps (Hanko/Inkan).
3. Rounded contrasting decorative elements: thin translucent rings, hollow data pills and discreet graphic circles contrasting with the straight edges of the blocks.

**Lighting \& Finish:** Raw matte concrete textures and translucent smoked glass, soft ambient occlusion, directional studio lighting without plastic reflections and without excessive flashy cyber neon glow.

**04.** **DESIGN SYSTEM .MD**

You are an expert front-end engineer. When building interfaces, pages and components in this project, you **MUST** strictly follow the guidelines and visual tokens below. Never use components with a generic framework look or saturated default themes.

**1.** **COLOR AND SURFACE TOKENS (STRICT PALETTE).**

1. **Canvas / General Background (surface-0):** #0d1216 (deep mineral black, matte finish).
2. **Panels / Cards (surface-1):** #141a1f (dark graphite with slight opacity).
3. **Interactive Surfaces / Hover (surface-2):** #1c242c.
4. **Primary Typography / Lines (text-primary):** #dedfd7 (crisp matte off-white).
5. **Secondary Typography (text-muted):** #7e8790 (soft mid gray).
6. **Structural Borders (border-hairline):** 1px solid rgba(222, 223, 215, 0.08).
7. **Accent Color (Highlight Color):** #E63946 (Vermilion/Hanko Red). Use it surgically on:
    1. Status dots and active badges.
    2. Technical underlines or occasional highlights.
    3. Decorative seals and primary buttons.

**2.** **TYPOGRAPHY AND HIERARCHY.**

1. **Primary Titles (Display):**
    1. Fonts: Heavy geometric sans-serif (e.g. Cabinet Grotesk, Archivo Black, Space Grotesk or Syne).
    2. Format: Uppercase, font-black or font-extrabold weight, tight tracking (tracking-tight).
    3. Dry Punctuation: Every main title must end with a full stop (e.g. "PROJECT.", "KDE WEBAPP.").
2. **Body and General UI:**
    1. Fonts: Geist, Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans, font-normal and font-medium weights.
3. **Metadata, Codes and Axes (Mono \& Oriental):**
    1. Fonts: Technical monospace (JetBrains Mono, Geist Mono).
    2. Labels and Tags: Dotted prefixes or short hashtags (In., Git., #01, \[ 0.1.0 \]).
    3. Vertical Typography / Oriental Characters: Use small vertical structural tags in kanji/katakana as guide lines and breathing room in the layout.

**3.** **STRUCTURE AND LAYOUT (BENTO GRID + 'MA' CONCEPT).**

1. **Rigid Bento Grid:** Clean modular structures with gap-4 or gap-6.
2. **Negative Space ('Ma'):** Keep generous breathing room (p-6 to p-8 inside cards), avoiding stacking unnecessary content.
3. **Borders and Corners:**
    1. Corners: Soft, modern rounding (rounded-xl or rounded-2xl), contrasting with the rigidity of the inner blocks.
    2. Divider lines: Thin 1px horizontal and vertical dividers.

**4.** **DECORATIVE MICRO-DETAILS AND GRAPHICS.**

1. **Technical Crosses (+):** Rhythmic alignment sequences in empty areas (e.g. + + + +) with low opacity.
2. **Dot Grids (Dot Matrix):** Subtle dot patterns (bg-radial-dots or small discreet dots . . .).
3. **Hanko Seals (Oriental Stamps):** Compact rectangular or square badges with a red border and translucent background.
4. **Circular Elements:** Thin translucent rings and hollow pills as decorative elements of visual contrast.

**5.** **ANIMATIONS AND INTERACTIONS.**

1. **Card Hover:** Subtle -2px elevation on the Y axis with a clean transition.

**Active Border:** Subtle border transition on hover (hover:border-white/20).
