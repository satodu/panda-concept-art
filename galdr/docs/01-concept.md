# 01. Concept

## In one sentence

Primitive Nordic cyberpunk, minimal: each crystal is a carved runestone read by a cold machine, a data shard lit by ice-colored light and described in short, serious prose.

> **The crystal is the concept, not a literal object.** It lives in the visual language: facets, 45° chamfers, refraction in the light, ice-colored glow. A literal crystal is one hero option among others (carved standing stone, rune tablet, floating rune ring, a small shard set in stone). Do not put a crystal in every image or prompt; use one only when the brief asks for it.

## The myth

**Galdr (Old Norse: the chanted spell of the runes).** Runes carved into crystal, read by cold machines. The product is a rune archive, not a faction. That sets the tone: terse, technical, ritual. Every screen is a **runestone plate**.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Means | Appears as |
|---|---|---|
| **Catalog** | everything has a number, locality, measurement | `GD-0417`, mono table, scale ruler |
| **Editorial** | magazine hierarchy | title, subtitle, body, caption, note (4+ levels) |
| **Facet** | cutting geometry | 45° chamfered corner, facet lines in the background, rhombus seal |
| **Light** | depth through light, not shadow | inner glow, rim light, frosted layers |

## Voice

Short, factual sentences. "Cluster of prismatic purple quartz crystals, collected from a basaltic geode." No slang, no exclamation marks.

## The waifu as seer

The character appears as a **seer portrait**: sober dark hooded coat with a thin silver rune clasp, small panel frame, restrained painting. Never as screaming key art with game effects. For events, use the "serious key art" prompt in `07-ai-prompts.md`.

## When to use

Catalogs, collections, archives, asset libraries, research dashboards, collection games, digital archives, premium products with a serious tone.

## When not to use

Light or casual products. The concept asks for careful reading.

## Difference from Oriental Brutalismo

| | Galdr | Oriental Brutalismo |
|---|---|---|
| Myth | primitive Nordic cyberpunk, rune archive | Japanese editorial brutalism |
| Accent | ice `#8FF3FF` | vermilion `#E63946` |
| Canvas | near black `#0C0C0B` | charcoal `#090a0f` |
| Corners | 45° chamfer | radius + hairline |
| Depth | light and frost | hairline |
| Layout | editorial grid | Bento |
